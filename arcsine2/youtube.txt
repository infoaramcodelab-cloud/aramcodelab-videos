TITLE
The Arc Sine Law: Why Fair Games Are So Unfair

DESCRIPTION
Two players toss a fair coin 1000 times. Surely each is ahead about half of the time? No. The most likely outcome is that one player leads almost the whole game. In this video we run the experiment, find the reason with the reflection trick, derive the exact formula, watch it turn into the arc sine law, and check it with ten lines of Python.
