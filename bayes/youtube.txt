TITLE
A Positive Test: Am I Ill? | Bayes’ Rule

DESCRIPTION
A hypothetical test catches 99% of people with a condition. Why can a positive result still mean a 50% chance of having it? We test a simulated crowd, count two kinds of positive results, turn the picture into Bayes’ rule, and check it with ten lines of Python. The example is about mathematics, not diagnosis.
